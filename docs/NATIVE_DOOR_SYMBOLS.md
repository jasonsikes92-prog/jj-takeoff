# Native door symbol recognition

The opening schedule includes `door_symbol_candidates` from native PDF strokes. It connects a printed numeric size and measured wall gap to a drawn leaf and segmented swing, paired hinged leaves, offset sliding panels or a partially retracted rectangular leaf inside a wall pocket. It runs when `opening_schedule.from_folder` reads a job with a plan PDF; `/api/opening-schedule` returns the evidence. This change does not add a visual editor control or restart existing review services.

The drawn leaf supplies the hinge and radius. Swing vertices must lie within 0.15 inch of that circle, turn consistently through 15–110 degrees, and have angular steps no larger than 12 degrees. Leaf length must agree with nominal width within 1 inch; the hinge and closed swing endpoint must be within 3 inches of the measured gap ends.

Pocket recognition requires two nominal-length cavity rails, a closed back, and a thinner four-sided leaf rectangle crossing the cavity mouth, with at least 20% of the leaf on each side. The cavity must extend behind a gap end and align with the wall centerline. Leaf thickness is 0.75–2 inches; cavity thickness is 1.5–4 inches. These are explicit symbol-recognition limits, not construction tolerances or code requirements. A lone rectangle, parallel wall lines or a size tag is insufficient. Fully closed/retracted pockets, other drafting conventions, raster drawings, cubic swings, folding symbols and untagged openings remain unsupported. The supported offset-panel pattern does not distinguish an exterior sliding unit from an interior bypass assembly.

Paired hinges require two half-width leaves hinged at opposite gap ends. Both swings must open to the same side and meet at their closed tips within 0.25 inch. Offset-panel recognition requires two closed rectangles on adjacent tracks, each approximately half the nominal width, partially overlapping, with one anchored near a jamb and both outer jambs drawn. Only supported visible solid strokes participate; PDF rectangle and quadrilateral commands supply their actual edges. These hypotheses can close room boundaries but do not decide exterior/interior role or authorize two separately purchased door units. See [paired-symbol verification](MULTI_PANEL_DOOR_INTERPRETATION.md).

Candidates carry plan, gap, scale, tag and stroke evidence hashes. Duplicate coincident strokes do not add doors; distinct competing symbols stay ambiguous. Contradictory or ambiguous geometry withholds a saved classification from current quantities while retaining the original decision under `conflicting_source_review`. It does not silently replace that decision with a new approved role. A changed measurement cannot make an old review current. Missing detection never means zero doors.
