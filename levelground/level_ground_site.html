Before you decide

Is it really worth it?

You're about to make the largest purchase of your life. If we surface even one lowball allowance or missing scope, the report has paid for itself many times over — and if we don't, it's free. The downside is a few hundred dollars; the upside is tens of thousands and a lot of sleep.

How accurate is it?

We don't claim a magic percentage. Quantities are measured straight off your plans. Missing scopes and lowball allowances are facts about your bid. Costs are given as honest ranges with the assumptions shown — never a fake-precise number. Every line is labeled so you know exactly how sure we are.

Will this make my builder mad?

A good builder welcomes an informed client. We're not here to bash builders — we are one. The report gives you better questions, not accusations, so you can have a smarter, calmer conversation before you sign.

What if I only have plans, no bid yet?

That's our Pre-Bid Report. We tell you what your home should cost and exactly what a complete, honest bid must include — so you're armed before you ever collect a quote.

Is my information private?

Yes. Your plans and bid are used only to build your report. We never sell your data, and we never take referral fees from builders. You're the only person we answer to.
